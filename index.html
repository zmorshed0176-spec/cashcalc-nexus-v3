What is FE CashCalc?

FE CashCalc is cashflow planning software for UK advisers. It brings your clients' financial situations to life, from their first goals to a full lifetime plan, in a way they can follow in the meeting.

Here's what you can do in FE CashCalc:

  • Map out life events and goals on a visual timeline
  • Create net and gross cashflow plans
  • Conduct both stochastic and deterministic stress testing
  • Run calculators for specific events, from early retirement to inheritance tax
  • Produce visual, client-friendly reports

FAQ

Questions advisers ask

Is FE CashCalc changing now it's in Nexus?

CashCalc stays the planner you know. What Nexus by FE fundinfo adds is the link to fund research in FE Analytics, so you can move between your cashflow plan and your research without switching systems.

What does Nexus add to FE CashCalc?

Investment recommendations from FE Analytics flow into FE CashCalc's tax-aware planning tools without re-entering data. You can base growth forecasts on the asset allocation of real client holdings rather than generic growth rates, and run Monte Carlo simulations on the actual portfolio.

Who is FE CashCalc for?

UK financial advisers, paraplanners and advice firms. Licences are per individual adviser, with a free paraplanner licence included, and larger firms can ask for group pricing.

How much does FE CashCalc cost?

£80/month + VAT per individual adviser licence. A paraplanner licence is free with every individual adviser licence. For larger firms, contact us for a bespoke group price.
